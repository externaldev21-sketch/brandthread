# App Store / Google Play launch readiness audit

Audited against the current Apple App Store Review Guidelines and, where noted, the
equivalent Google Play policy. Scope: `artifacts/mobile` (the shipping app). Snapshot:
`dev` as checked out on `claude/stoic-dirac-gehy4g`, 2026-09-24.

This audit builds on, and does not repeat, the existing groundwork:
- [`docs/app-store/release-flow.md`](../app-store/release-flow.md) — build/submit process, review notes template
- [`docs/app-store/ipad-release-checklist.md`](../app-store/ipad-release-checklist.md) — iPad correctness, screen by screen
- [`docs/app-store/privacy-labels.md`](../app-store/privacy-labels.md) — App Store Connect privacy answers, kept in sync with the privacy manifest by `scripts/legal-privacy.test.ts`
- [`docs/polish/punch-list.md`](../polish/punch-list.md) — a much larger line-by-line UI/UX audit; most of its P0s (invisible buttons, fake data, placeholder legal text, dead controls) are already marked ✅ FIXED there. This audit does not re-list those; see that doc for the small number still ⏳ NOT FIXED (money-movement UI polish, not blockers below).

**Legend:** PASS — compliant, no action. RISK — compliant-ish but a reviewer could reasonably object; needs a decision or reviewer note. FAIL — will likely be rejected as-is.

## Summary

- **21 of 26 rows PASS outright.** The team's prior polish pass (`docs/polish/punch-list.md`) already closed almost every code-level App Store blocker this audit looked for: legal placeholder text, invisible/dead buttons, missing report/block wiring, the Apple Sign In logo, permission strings, and the privacy manifest are all in good shape.
- **1 genuine FAIL requiring a product decision, not a quick fix:** row 10, **Boost / Create-ad paid promotion features run through Stripe instead of Apple/Google IAP.** This is the single highest-risk item for this launch — see the dedicated section in `app-store-metadata.md` review notes and the flag in the PR description.
- **2 documentation gaps closed by this PR:** demo reviewer accounts (row 22) and the iPad checklist execution reminder (row 23) — the checklist itself already existed, it just needs to be run.
- **Everything else is a RISK worth a one-line reviewer note**, not a blocker: freelancer marketplace payments (row 11) and the lack of an automated content filter beyond human moderation (row 5).
