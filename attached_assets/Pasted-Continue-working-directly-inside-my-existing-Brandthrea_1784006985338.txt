INVENTORY DEFINITIONS

Use these definitions consistently:

Available:
Units that can currently be sold.

On hand:
Physical units currently at a location.

Reserved:
Units assigned to paid or confirmed orders but not yet shipped.

Incoming:
Units expected from production, transfer, return inspection, or restock.

Committed pre-order:
Units promised to customers but not yet available as physical inventory.

Damaged:
Units that cannot be sold normally.

Unavailable:
Units blocked for quality review, internal use, or another reason.

Available quantity should be calculated safely using the correct stock state.

Do not let the Seller manually create impossible combinations such as negative on-hand stock without a valid reason or override.
