Delete your Brandthread account

Use this page to permanently delete your Brandthread account without opening the app. You can also do it in the app under Settings, Delete account.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, username, photo and bio
  • Posts, comments, stories, likes, reposts and follows
  • Direct messages you sent
  • Saved items, cart, addresses and notification settings
  • For sellers: your storefront, product listings, discount codes and shipping settings
  • Your sign-in on every device

What we keep

  • Order, payment, refund and tax records, with your name and address removed, for as long as the law requires
  • Reports you made about other people's content, without your identity

Open orders, funds held for drops and unresolved disputes must be settled first. If any apply, we tell you what to do.
