# Seller Agreement

Version: 2026-09-23

## Agreement

This Seller Agreement applies to you when you open a store, list products, run a drop or accept payouts on Brandthread. It supplements our [Terms of Service](/terms), [Community Guidelines](/community-guidelines) and [Refund Policy](/refund-policy). If these documents conflict on a selling matter, this Seller Agreement applies. By opening a store or continuing to sell, you agree to it.

## Eligibility

- You must be at least 18 years old and able to form a binding contract. If you sell for a business, you confirm you have authority to bind it.
- You must complete Stripe Connect onboarding, including identity, business, tax and bank details, before you can receive payouts. Brandthread keeps your verification status, not your identity documents.
- You must keep your store and payout information accurate and up to date. Team members you add act only within the permissions you grant, and you are responsible for their activity.

## Fees

- Brandthread charges a platform fee of 5% of each marketplace order’s subtotal, meaning item prices after discounts and excluding shipping and sales tax. The fee is deducted automatically when the order is paid, or when held drop funds are released to you.
- Stripe charges payment processing fees under its own terms.

- When an order is refunded, Brandthread returns its platform fee on the refunded portion. Stripe does not return its processing fee, so that cost stays with the sale.
- Seller subscription plans (Starter, Growth and Pro) are billed monthly. Plan prices and limits are shown before you subscribe. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel through the billing provider you subscribed with. Cancelling stops future renewals, and your plan stays active to the end of the paid period.
- Optional paid features, such as boosts and ads, show their price before you pay.
- Fees that apply to manufacturers and freelancers are shown before you pay them.

## Payouts and held funds

- Payouts are made through Stripe Connect to your connected bank account. Stripe may require more information, and may delay, hold or reverse payouts under its terms.
- For in-stock orders, your share of the payment is transferred to your Stripe account after the platform fee is deducted.
- For preorders and drops, buyer payments are held by Brandthread and released to you one order at a time when you add shipment tracking for that order. You may use held funds only through Brandthread’s approved flows, such as paying a Brandthread manufacturer for production.
- If a drop is cancelled or isn’t fulfilled by its fulfillment deadline, every unshipped order is refunded to the buyer automatically from the held funds. The deadline can be no more than 180 days away.
- You authorize Brandthread and Stripe to deduct refunds, chargebacks, disputes, platform fees and any other amounts you owe from your balance or future payouts. If held funds have already been spent and a refund is owed, the shortfall shows on the drop and is owed by you.
- You are responsible for collecting and reporting taxes that apply to your sales.

## Orders, shipping and returns

- You are the seller of record. You are responsible for describing products accurately, fulfilling orders on time, packaging, shipping, customer service and product safety.
- Give realistic ship dates for preorders and drops, and update buyers about delays.
- Publish a return and cancellation policy on your store. You must honor it, and you must follow the Refund Policy and applicable consumer law.
- Respond to return requests promptly. An approved return is refunded to the buyer, for the full order or the amount you approve, through Brandthread.
- When a buyer opens a payment dispute, you can respond with evidence or accept it from your dashboard within the deadline shown. Missing the deadline can mean the dispute is decided against you.

## Prohibited items and conduct

- Counterfeits, knock-offs presented as genuine, stolen designs, and anything that infringes another person’s trademark, copyright or other rights.
- Weapons, drugs and drug paraphernalia, recalled or unsafe products, and anything illegal to sell or ship where you or your buyer are located.
- Adult content and sexually explicit products, and anything that breaks the Community Guidelines.
- Listings that don’t match what is delivered.
- Taking payment for a sale that started on Brandthread anywhere else, asking buyers to pay by Zelle, Cash App, wire, crypto or gift cards, fake reviews and bought engagement.
