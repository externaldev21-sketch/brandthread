## Fees

- Joining Brandthread and listing your factory is free. There is no listing or subscription fee for manufacturers.
- When a seller pays one of your order cards, Brandthread keeps a platform fee of 5% of the card total.
- Payment processing is passed through at cost: the card network fee for card payments, or 0.8% capped at US$5 for bank (ACH) payments.
- The platform fee and processing are deducted before your payout. The order card shows the rate and the amount you receive before you send it.
- If an order is refunded, Brandthread returns its platform fee on the refunded amount. Processing fees are not returned by the payment provider.

## Payments stay on Brandthread

- Every order with a seller you met or worked with on Brandthread must be priced and paid through a Brandthread order card.
- You must not ask for, accept or suggest payment outside Brandthread for those orders, including bank or wire transfer, T/T, PayPal, Wise, Zelle, Cash App, Venmo, Western Union, Alipay, WeChat Pay, crypto or cash.
- Orders paid outside Brandthread aren't protected: no payment protection, dispute help or order tracking.
- Before a seller's first paid order with you, Brandthread hides email addresses, phone numbers, messaging app IDs, website links and payment details in messages and on your profile. These rules apply to sellers too.

## Non-circumvention

- For 12 months after a seller is introduced to you through Brandthread (through the directory, a request for quote, a quote request, a message thread or an invite), you agree not to take orders from that seller, or anyone acting for that seller, outside Brandthread.
- The 12 months restart from the seller's most recent paid order with you on Brandthread.
- This does not apply to a seller you already worked with before Brandthread, if you can show that relationship. A seller who invited you to Brandthread from an existing relationship is treated as already known to you.
- If you break this section, Brandthread may charge the platform fee that would have applied to the orders taken outside Brandthread, deduct it from your payouts, and suspend or close your profile.
