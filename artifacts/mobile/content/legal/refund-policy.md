# Refund Policy

Version: 2026-09-23

## Overview

This policy explains how cancellations, returns and refunds work when you buy on Brandthread. It is part of our [Terms of Service](/terms). Sellers, not Brandthread, are the sellers of the products in their stores, so each seller’s own return and cancellation policy, shown on their store, applies in addition to this policy and to your legal rights.

## Purchase protection

- Every checkout on Brandthread is paid through Brandthread. Keep your conversation and payment on Brandthread so we can help if something goes wrong.
- If an order doesn’t arrive, isn’t as described, or a seller can’t ship it, contact the seller first from your order. If you can’t resolve it, contact Brandthread support and we’ll review it.
- Funds for preorders and drops are held by Brandthread until the seller ships your order, which is what lets us refund you if the drop doesn’t happen.
- Refunds always go back to the original payment method. If you paid any part with Thread Cash, that part returns to your Thread Cash balance.

## Cancelling an order

- You can cancel an order in the app from your order page before it ships. The refund is issued first, and the order is only marked cancelled once the refund is confirmed.
- A preorder can be cancelled while its drop is still collecting orders. Once the seller has started production, the money for the drop has been spent, so the order can no longer be cancelled from the app.
- If a seller cancels your order, you receive a full refund.
- Purchase rewards earned on an order are reversed when the order is refunded.

## Returns

- You can request a return from your order page once the order has shipped. Choose the items, pick a reason, say whether you want a refund, exchange, store credit or replacement, and add up to 5 photos.
- Only one return request can be open for an order at a time. You can submit a new request if the earlier one was denied.
- The seller reviews your request and approves or denies it, and you are notified by email of the outcome. The seller’s return policy, including any return window and who pays for return shipping, determines what is accepted.

- When a return is approved, the seller issues the refund for the full order or for the amount they approve. The refund goes to your original payment method and usually appears within a few business days, depending on your bank.

## Preorders and drops

- You see an estimated ship date before you pay for a preorder or drop. Estimates can change, and sellers must keep you updated about delays.
- Your payment is held by Brandthread and released to the seller one order at a time when the seller adds shipment tracking.
- If a drop is cancelled, or it isn’t fulfilled by its fulfillment deadline, every unshipped order is refunded in full automatically. You don’t need to ask. The deadline is set by the seller from the estimated ship date and can be no more than 180 days away.

- Once your preorder ships, it follows the return process above.

## Payment disputes

- If you don’t recognise a charge or something is wrong with an order, please contact the seller and Brandthread support first so we can fix it quickly.
- You can also dispute a charge with your card issuer. Disputes are decided by your bank through Stripe. The seller can respond with evidence or accept the dispute.

## Cases we review

- Counterfeit, unsafe or illegal items, and listings that break our Community Guidelines, can be reported from the listing. We may refund buyers from held funds and remove the seller.
- Orders that don’t arrive, arrive damaged, or differ significantly from the listing are reviewed by our support team when the seller and buyer can’t resolve them.
