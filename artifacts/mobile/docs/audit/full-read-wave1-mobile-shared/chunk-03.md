## Fixes
- `components/buy-now/BuyNowFlow.tsx:149` — Buy Now opened Stripe's hosted page with `openBrowserAsync` and treated any close (`cancel`/`dismiss`) as "Payment was cancelled." without asking Stripe. Buyers who pay and tap Done (or whose redirect doesn't close an in-app browser) were told the payment was cancelled while an order existed — a double-charge risk. Also a thrown `verifySession` after payment fell into the outer catch and said "We could not start secure checkout". → Uses `openAuthSessionAsync(url, currentCheckoutReturnUrls().redirectUrl)` and `hostedCheckoutVerdict` from lib/checkoutReturn.ts, as app/buyer-checkout.tsx does: it always verifies (6 attempts after a success redirect, 2 otherwise), says "cancelled" only when Stripe shows no payment, and reports a verify failure as "still confirming". Test: `components/buy-now/BuyNowFlow.audit.test.tsx` (failed before: yes, 3 of 4. The 4th, real-cancel case, guards against regressions)
- `components/buy-now/BuyNowFlow.tsx:186` — order success passed `paymentMethodLabel: 'Card on file'`, a made-up value. The buyer paid on Stripe's hosted page, maybe with Apple Pay or a new card.
