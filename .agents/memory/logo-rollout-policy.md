---
name: Logo rollout scope
description: Asset-only Brandthread logo replacements preserve current UI sizing and placement.
---

Use the owner's approved logo PNG byte-for-byte for the canonical logo.
Do not resize, recolor or reinterpret that master. Regenerate other raster
copies at their existing dimensions, preserving transparency and launcher
background/padding treatments. Historical documentation captures are excluded.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested replacement artwork, not a redesign:
"keep the same sizing of all my current logos, just replace it."

**How to apply:** Leave component sizes, positions, layouts and animations
unchanged. The owner approved removing only forced logo tints so the chrome
remains visible, and reconstructing backgrounds behind baked-in themed marks
while retaining their treatment and padding. Do not use those exceptions to
change any other styling or animation.
