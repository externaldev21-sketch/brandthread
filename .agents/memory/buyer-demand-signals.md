---
name: Buyer demand signals
description: Truth and presentation rules for buyer-facing product and drop demand data
---

**Rule:** High Demand contains only product-backed rows that qualify through real demand or low remaining stock. Show paid claimed units and current remaining stock as separate values; never fabricate a historical total capacity. Show countdowns only for persisted end times.

**Why:** Engagement-ranked posts, mutable stock, and order counts cannot safely stand in for product demand, units sold, or original supply. Mislabeling ordinary inventory creates false scarcity.

**How to apply:** Keep demand styling buyer-only. Use the user-scoped signature accent only for true urgency/status. Keep ordinary post trends in a separately labeled Trending section, and show an honest empty state when no product qualifies.
