---
name: Onboarding value-first contract
description: Smart defaults, early seller ownership, and pre-plan AI sample rules
---

**Rule:** Onboarding may preselect sensible questionnaire answers, but every default remains editable and draft-safe. Seller onboarding includes storefront accent ownership and offers one optional successful AI logo sample before plan commitment.

**Why:** Defaults reduce unnecessary decisions, personalization creates meaningful investment, and sellers should experience real product value before being asked to start a paid trial. AI downtime must never block onboarding or consume the promised sample.

**How to apply:** Persist choices per Clerk user. Enforce the AI allowance on the server with a reclaimable reservation lease; only a successful generation permanently consumes it, and completion must match the active reservation token.
